2. How a day is decided

SOP p.6 names the statuses. SOP p.8 says a non-working day is justified only by an Approved request of the matching type for that rider on that exact date. Live data uses Kuwait calendar days.

Priority (first match wins) Cell Live source Approved request required?
1. Day after Kuwait today blank — —
2. Check-in that Kuwait day 12 (grey) attendance_logs.check_in_at No. A check-in wins over leave on the same day
3. Accident cover Accident (light green) Leave type Accident, or Sick Leave subtype Accident, covering that date Yes — Approved or awaiting rider acknowledgement
4. Other sick leave Sick (orange) Sick Leave covering that date Yes
5. Other leave OFF (green) Leave covering that date Yes
6. Otherwise, day ≤ today Absent (red) No check-in and no covering request No — counts against the rider
Which month a request belongs to. SOP p.2 / p.8: the request must match that same date, not the day it was filed. The queue and the day grid both use the request’s start date → end date. A sick leave filed in September that covers 25–28 August appears in August. A leave filed in September that runs 15 November–15 January does not appear in September.

Worked day (status 12)

SOP p.6: “Worked a 12-hour shift.” Total Hours on p.7 / p.16 is count(12) × 12 — a standard shift, not clock hours. Live payroll treats 12 as a Kuwait-day check-in. A rider who clocked in with zero deliveries still shows 12. Performance’s “working day = verified orders > 0” is a different report and is not used here.

4. How to verify — open the SOP beside this list

Sign in → Operations → Payroll & Requests, or open https://dpdadmin-prod.vercel.app/en/payroll. Default tab is Payroll. Default month is the current Kuwait month.

4.1 Shared chrome — SOP pages 3–4

  1. Confirm the page title is Payroll & Requests and the two tabs are Payroll and Requests.
  2. Count the month buttons: exactly three (current + previous two). There is no date-range picker and no older archive.
  3. Switch to August, then July. The day headers must change (1-Aug … 31-Aug, then 1-Jul …) and the footer must show Fixed Days = month days − 2 (31 → 29, 30 → 28).
  4. Confirm the seven slicers: Zone, Partner, Vehicle, Nationality, Source type, Company, Store. Each has search and All.
  5. Store depends on Partner (SOP p.4). Open Partner, pick only Keeta. Store must read “No stores for the selected partner”. Restaurant column must be (Pool). Clear filters. Pick Americana — Store must list stores again.
  6. Click Clear filters. Every slicer returns to All. The efficiency drill-down (if any) also clears.

4.2 Payroll KPIs and grid — SOP pages 5–7

  1. Five cards, in this order: Riders in payroll · Total active riders · Avg attendance eff. · Riders at / above 100% · Riders with unjustified days.
  2. Legend colours: 12 grey, OFF green, Sick orange, Accident light green, Absent red. No Travelled and no Cancelled chips.
  3. Identity columns: AM ID (employee ID), MG ID (driver code), Name, Restaurant, Zone, Partner, Nationality, Status (Active / Inactive).
  4. One column per calendar day of the selected month, then Total Days, Total Hours, OFF, Sick, Accident, Absence, Fixed Days, Efficiency%.
  5. Pick one rider. Total Days = count of 12. Total Hours = Total Days × 12. Efficiency% = Total Days ÷ Fixed Days × 100 (one decimal on screen). Green at 100%+, red below 80%.
  6. A future day (after Kuwait today) is blank — not Absent, not in the totals.

4.3 Efficiency and justification — SOP page 8

  1. September 2026 is a 30-day month → Fixed Days 28. August and July are 31-day months → Fixed Days 29.
  2. Efficiency is not capped. A rider who works more than Fixed Days reads above 100% and is counted in “Riders at / above 100%”.
  3. OFF / Sick / Accident without an Approved request increment “Riders with unjustified days”. Absent does not.
  4. A request still Pending or Under review does not justify the day (SOP p.15 critical note).
  5. Same-date rule. Open August → Requests. RCM-0092 (Sick Leave 25–28 August, filed 8 September) must appear in August and must not appear in September. RCM-0085 (Leave 15 November–15 January, filed 4 September) must appear in neither September nor August.

4.4 Distribution chart and exports — SOP pages 9–11

  1. Five bars: ≥100%, 90–100%, 80–90%, 70–80%, <70%. Their counts must add up to “Riders in payroll”.
  2. Click the largest bar (usually <70%). A green banner appears: “Showing only riders in the … bucket — N rider(s)” plus Show all. The table footer “Showing X of Y” must match N.
  3. Click the same bar again — drill-down clears. Click another bar, then change month — drill-down clears.
  4. Download this table (and header Export to Excel) saves the current view, including the drill-down. Filename MGGO-payroll-YYYY-MM.csv. No Travelled / Cancelled columns.
  5. Chart CSV downloads every rider in the filtered month, grouped by bucket, with a Bucket column and Unjustified Days. Filename MGGO-efficiency-distribution-YYYY-MM.csv.
  6. Apply Partner = Keeta, then export. The file must contain only Keeta riders — never rows you cannot see on screen (SOP p.11).

4.5 Requests tab — SOP pages 12–15

  1. Eight tiles: Leave, Sick Leave, Accident, Asset, Fuel, Loan, Documents, Salary Justification. Red badge = Pending + Under review for that type.
  2. Status chips: All · Pending · Under review · Approved · Rejected. Type AND status combine. Queue sorts Pending first.
  3. Click Leave, then Pending. Only that intersection remains. Click the Leave tile again to clear the type.
  4. Only Leave / Sick Leave / Accident justify a payroll day. Asset, Fuel, Loan, Documents and Salary Justification do not (SOP p.13).
  5. Fuel tile includes Fuel Refunds (same Fleet queue). Accident tile is Leave-Accident or Sick-Leave-Accident — there is no separate Accident request type.
  6. Row columns: Request ID, Rider, ID, Type, Day, Zone, Partner, Reviewing dept., Status. Click a row — it opens the existing request record (approve / reject / clarify stay there).
  7. Approval Workflow panel (right): four SOP steps, verbatim, including “Safety & Legal” in step 3. Below the steps: Awaiting action and Requests / rider (one decimal). Changing month or a slicer updates both. Tile / status chips do not shrink these two numbers.
  8. Reviewing dept. on a live row is the current approval step (Reporting Manager / HR / Payroll / Fleet), not the SOP ideal label.
