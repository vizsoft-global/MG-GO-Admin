Outsourced company DPD target and incentive

Audience: operations / payroll stakeholders  ·  Admin live on production  ·  Rider Play Store update not published yet

1. What you requested

Outsourced riders should not always follow restaurant Extra Earnings quests. Each supplying company should be able to run its own daily target and a flat per-order incentive.

  • Own daily DPD target per outsourced company (not the restaurant rule).
  • Simple scheme: pay extra for every verified order above the target; deduct for every verified order below the target.
  • Sadeeq live from day one: target 15, +0.100 KD per order above, −0.350 KD per order below.
  • Other outsourced companies: scheme stays off until you turn it on (so nobody is paid or deducted by surprise).
  • In-house (MG) and Unassigned outsourced riders keep today’s restaurant delivery / incentive rules.
  • The rider Daily DPD card should use that company target. The card may count orders as they are submitted; payout, lock, remaining, and achieved stay on verified orders only.
  • Staff must be able to set target, on/off, rates, and start date in Admin — without a developer change.

2. What we delivered

  • Settings → Companies now shows DPD target and a Scheme badge. Edit opens target, emerald On toggle, above/below KD rates, effective-from date, and a live preview of incentive / deduction / net.
  • The in-house system company (MG) stays locked. It has no company scheme — in-house riders keep restaurant rules.
  • Sadeeq is seeded On (15 / +0.100 / −0.350) from the Kuwait date the change went live (30 Sep 2026).
  • Other outsourced companies are seeded with incentive Off from that same day. You can turn a scheme on when ready.
  • A live scheme replaces restaurant Extra Earnings quests for that rider on Home and Extra Earnings.
  • Home “This week” delivery counts now follow the working shift day (overnight after midnight still belongs to the shift that started).
  • Admin production: https://dpdadmin-prod.vercel.app/en/settings/source-companies
  • Rider app: screens are built. They will show for riders only after the Play Store update. Until then, production Play still shows restaurant Extra Earnings.

How the scheme pays (verified orders today = n, target = T)

  • If n > T: incentive = (n − T) × above rate. Deduction = 0.
  • If n < T: deduction = (T − n) × below rate. Incentive = 0. Zero orders = a full-day deduction (T × below).
  • If n = T: incentive and deduction are both 0.

3. How to verify — Admin

Sign in at https://dpdadmin-prod.vercel.app with a staff account that can open Settings. Open Settings → Companies (or /en/settings/source-companies).

  1. The table has a DPD column. Sadeeq should show T 15 and a green Scheme badge.
  2. Other outsourced companies should not show Scheme unless you already turned them on. DPD may still show a target if one was set.
  3. The MG / in-house company shows a lock and “—” for DPD (no company scheme).
  4. Click Edit on Sadeeq. Confirm DPD target 15, Scheme On (green with a check), above 0.100, below 0.350, and an effective-from date.
  5. The preview table updates as you type. At 30 / 21 / 15 / 10 / 5 / 0 orders it should match the example table above.
  6. Turn Scheme off on a test company (not Sadeeq if riders are live), save, then on again. List badge follows On / Off.
  7. Scheme On with a blank target, blank rate, or no effective-from date is refused with an error — it must not save.
  8. You cannot deactivate a company that still has active drivers.
  9. Optional: Performance → DPD / Outsource — outsourced riders on a live scheme use the company target for DPD, not the restaurant target.

4. How to verify — Rider app (User)

Play Store is not updated yet. Production riders still see restaurant Extra Earnings until Play is published. Use a test build from Vizsoft, or wait for the Play release note.

Outsourced rider on a live scheme (e.g. Sadeeq)

  1. Home Daily DPD Target uses the company target (15 for Sadeeq), not a restaurant number.
  2. The card count can rise when the rider submits an order (in transit / pending). Remaining / achieved still wait for verified.
  3. Home Extra Earnings shows the company card (company name, daily target, verified today, +rate, −rate, Incentive / Deduction / Net) — not restaurant “quest” rows.
  4. Open Extra Earnings from the menu. Same company card. No restaurant offer list.

In-house or Unassigned rider

  1. Home Daily DPD still follows the restaurant / zone target.
  2. Extra Earnings still lists restaurant incentive quests (Locked / Active bands as before).
  3. No company scheme card.

If a company’s scheme is Off, those outsourced riders also stay on restaurant Extra Earnings until you turn the scheme On and the effective-from date has been reached (Kuwait calendar).
