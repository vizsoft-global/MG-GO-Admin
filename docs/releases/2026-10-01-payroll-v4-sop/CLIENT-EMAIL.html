Hi team,

The MGGO Payroll SOP v4.0 is now live on the admin panel. Payroll is no longer one hard-coded rule — each client owns its own rules, and your staff can change them on screen without a developer.

What you asked for, in short

  • The client is the unit: each client keeps its own hour values, thresholds, a default day status, and its own ordered list of day rules.
  • A rule matches on zone band (Good / Average / Low), zone name, orders and worked hours. The first match from the top wins.
  • The SOP result codes, exactly as written: Full day 12 h, Reduced day 3 h, Half day 6 h, Actual hours, Absent, Absent LH, Absent LO, Custom hours.
  • Zone efficiency is read from the previous completed month only: DPD = orders ÷ rider-days, efficiency = DPD ÷ the target DPD, banded Good ≥ 110%, Average ≥ 70%, Low < 70%. A zone nobody measured is Not set — never "Low".
  • One stated order of precedence: hand adjustment → Operations (OFF / Sick / Accident / Vehicle issue) → first matching client rule → client default → the previous behaviour.
  • Off Structure per rider (contracted OFF days, default 2) drives Required Hours, and is uploadable in bulk from Excel.
  • The Combined grid behaves like a spreadsheet: select a block of rider-days, fill it, copy and paste across it, and adjust the whole selection with one reason.
  • Attendance Efficiency = Actual Worked Hours ÷ Required Hours, uncapped, with filters.

What is live today

  • Admin: https://dpdadmin-prod.vercel.app/en/payroll — four pages: Payroll · Combined Payroll · Attendance & Orders · Settings, plus Requests.
  • Two SOP clients are seeded with their starting rule lists: Americana (zone band + orders) and Keeta (orders + hours).
  • Settings is where you edit it all: client criteria, the rule list, a Try the rules simulator that saves nothing, the Zones & efficiency table with a Recompute button and per-zone overrides, and a change log that names who changed what and when.
  • Combined Payroll has the grid, the batch adjustment (a reason is mandatory) and the manual changes record — original value, new value, reason, user, time.
  • Off Structure has a per-rider editor and a bulk Excel upload with a preview that says what will apply, what will not change and what was rejected, row by row.

No rider app update is needed. Payroll is an admin screen only. Nothing changes in the rider app. What the rider already does — clock in and out, deliver orders, raise Leave / Sick / Accident requests — is what feeds payroll, and the rules read those same figures.

A note on zone efficiency

Efficiency always comes from the previous completed month. That is deliberate: a band that moved with the current, half-finished month would silently re-price days you have already decided. So a newly created zone stays Not set until a full month has passed and you press Recompute. And a zone with no rider-days is left out of the target rather than dragging it down, which is why an empty zone cannot push a working zone into "Average".
