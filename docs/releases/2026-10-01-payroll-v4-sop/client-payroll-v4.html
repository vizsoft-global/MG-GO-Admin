Payroll v4 — client-owned rules, zone efficiency, and a spreadsheet grid

Audience: payroll / operations stakeholders  ·  Admin live on production  ·  No rider app update required  ·  Module: Payroll & Requests

1. What you requested (MGGO Payroll SOP v4.0)

Payroll in v3 classified a day with one hard-coded rule. The SOP makes the client the unit of the calculation, so each client can keep its own arrangement without a code change.

  • The client owns the rules. Each client keeps its own hour values, thresholds, a default day status, and its own ordered list of day rules.
  • Rules decide a day. A rule matches on zone band (Good / Average / Low), zone name, orders and worked hours; the first match from the top wins.
  • The SOP's own result codes: Full day 12 h, Reduced day 3 h, Half day 6 h, Actual hours, Absent, Absent LH, Absent LO, and Custom hours.
  • Zone efficiency comes from the previous completed month only — DPD = orders ÷ rider-days, efficiency = DPD ÷ the target DPD, banded Good / Average / Low.
  • A stated order of precedence: hand adjustment → Operations (OFF / Sick / Accident / Vehicle issue) → first matching client rule → client default → the old classification.
  • Off Structure per rider — contracted OFF days for the month (default 2) — drives Required Hours, and must be uploadable in bulk.
  • Excel-style grid: select a block of rider-days, fill, copy and paste across the block, and adjust the whole selection with one reason.
  • Attendance Efficiency = Actual Worked Hours ÷ Required Hours, uncapped, with status filters.
  • Everything above editable by your own staff — no developer, no deployment.

2. What we delivered

  • Payroll is now data, not code. Clients, their hour values, their default status and their rule lists are stored in the database and edited on screen.
  • Two SOP clients are seeded and ready: Americana (zone band + orders) and Keeta (orders + hours), each with the SOP starting rule list for the current month.
  • A stated precedence in one place — a hand adjustment always beats a rule, and Operations always beats a rule, in the grid, the export, the Attendance & Orders tab and the simulator alike.
  • Zone efficiency from last month, with a Recompute button and an Ops override for DPD, target and band that never destroys the automatic figure.
  • Off Structure per rider, plus bulk upload from an Excel sheet with a downloadable template and a preview that says what will apply and what will be skipped.
  • The Combined grid behaves like a spreadsheet — drag to select, corner handle to fill, copy / paste / fill down, and a reason is required before any hand adjustment lands.
  • A manual changes record — every hand adjustment keeps the original value, the new value, the reason, the user and the time, and a later recalculation never overwrites it.
  • A change log for every rule, threshold, band override and client edit, with your name and the time.
  • Four pages: Payroll · Combined Payroll · Attendance & Orders · Settings (plus Requests).

3. How a day is decided, in order

#SourceWhat it means
1Hand adjustmentA named person changed that rider-day and left a reason. Nothing overrides this.
2OperationsAn approved OFF / Sick / Accident / Vehicle-issue request covers the day. The day is that status and credits 0 hours.
3First matching client ruleThe client's own list, read top to bottom. The first rule whose conditions all hold decides the day.
4Client defaultNo rule matched. The client's own default status applies (Americana: Full day 12 h · Keeta: Actual hours).
5Previous behaviourOnly when the client has no saved rules at all for that month: a check-in is a full 12 h day, a covered day is that cover, anything else is Absent.

Result codes and the hours they credit: Full day · 12 h → 12 h · Reduced day · 3 h → 3 h · Half day · 6 h → 6 h · Actual hours → the logged check-in → check-out hours · Custom hours → the number typed · Absent / Absent LH / Absent LO → 0 h. Hours are the client's own values, so they can differ per client.

4. Zone efficiency — what the band means

DPD = zone orders ÷ rider-days  ·  Target DPD = the average DPD across the zones that actually had rider-days  ·  Zone efficiency % = DPD ÷ Target DPD × 100.

It is always read from the previous completed month. That is deliberate: a band that moved with the current, half-finished month would silently re-price days you have already decided.

Zone efficiencyBandDefault threshold
at or aboveGood110%
at or aboveAverage70%
belowLow< 70%
no previous-month dataNot set—

A zone nobody measured is Not set, never "Low". A zone with no rider-days has no DPD to average, so it is left out of the target instead of dragging it down. A zone that is Not set matches no seeded rule, so its days fall to the client default — an unmeasured zone is never punished for being unmeasured.

Worked example (Kuwait zones)

ZoneOrdersRider-daysDPDEfficiencyBand
Zone A1,2004030.00130.4%Good
Zone B4002516.0069.6%Low
Zone C00——Not set

Target DPD = (30.00 + 16.00) ÷ 2 = 23.00. Had the empty Zone C been counted as 0, the target would have fallen to 15.33 and Zone B would have read Average — which is exactly the distortion the rule avoids.

7. How to verify — Admin

Sign in at https://dpdadmin-prod.vercel.app and open Payroll & Requests (/en/payroll). You need a role holding payroll.view; edit rights need payroll.manage and the export needs payroll.export.

A. The month and the filters

  1. Five tabs are present: Payroll · Combined Payroll · Attendance & Orders · Requests · Settings.
  2. Range pills: This month · Last month · Custom. Custom accepts a real From / To range, but only inside this month and the previous two.
  3. The Partner view row lists each client with its rider count. Selecting one narrows the page — and the client is the same thing as the rider's Partner.
  4. Zone category chips (Good / Average / Low / Not set) filter the riders the same way.
  5. The summary strip reads Partner · riders · Good / Avg / Low / Not set · rules use zone + orders.
  6. Clicking a legend chip (Full day, Absent, 3 h …) filters the page to riders who have that day; the share card appears; clicking again clears it.

B. The Payroll tab

  1. KPI row: Riders in payroll · Total active riders · Avg attendance eff. · Riders at / above 100% · Riders with unjustified days · 3 h Days · Manual Adjustments.
  2. Per-rider columns include Off Structure, Required Hours, Actual Hours, Efficiency%, Zone band, and a count per day type.
  3. Column filters work per column (text, a list with an explicit All, and a min/max range) and Clear column filters resets them.
  4. Export to Excel downloads the filtered rows exactly as shown.

C. Combined Payroll — the spreadsheet grid

  1. Hovering the grid shows the hint: drag across cells to select · drag the corner handle to fill · Ctrl+C, Ctrl+V, Ctrl+D.
  2. Drag across a block of a rider's days. The selection bar reads N cells selected · R riders × D days.
  3. Drag the green corner handle downwards to fill the pattern into the cells you drag over. The target cells show a dashed preview before you release.
  4. Select a block and press Ctrl+C, then select another block and Ctrl+V. A smaller copied block tiles across the selected block. Ctrl+D fills down. Esc clears the selection.
  5. Select several cells and press Apply. The adjustment dialog opens with the cell / rider / day counts, the new status, and a Reason box.
  6. Apply with an empty reason is refused. The reason is mandatory for every manual adjustment.
  7. Choose Keep selected status to leave the cells exactly as the rules computed them, or pick a status (Full day 12 h, Reduced 3 h, Half day 6 h, Actual, OFF, Absent LH / LO, Sick, Accident, Vehicle issue, Custom hours).
  8. After applying, the changed cells carry an orange marker, and the Manual changes record shows the date/time, the user, the rider, the day, the original value, the adjusted value and the reason.
  9. Press Recompute / reload the month. The hand-adjusted cells must not change — a later recalculation never overwrites a manual adjustment.

D. Off Structure

  1. The header button Off Structure opens the dialog titled Contracted OFF days for <month>.
  2. Pick one rider, set the days and Save. The line under the picker shows the current value and its source (default / manual / bulk upload).
  3. Download template, fill a sheet with a Driver ID (or Employee Number) column and an Off days column, then Upload employee sheet.
  4. The preview lists every row with its verdict: Will apply · No change · Missing ID · Duplicate · Invalid off days · Exceeds month · Unknown ID · Ambiguous ID · Wrong month.
  5. Apply valid rows writes only the real changes. A sheet that changes nothing answers No Off Structure changes to write. Default OFF = 2 stays implicit.
  6. Set a rider to 4 OFF and re-check the Payroll tab: Required Hours drops by 4 × 12 = 48 h and Efficiency rises. The day cells do not move.

E. Settings — the rules engine

  1. Open Settings. The two seeded clients appear with their criteria ticks and their rule list for the month.
  2. The rule table reads as sentences — Zone band is one of Good, Average · Orders is less than 7 → Reduced day · 3 h — with the credited hours beside each result.
  3. Add rule, add conditions, reorder with the up/down arrows, and Save rules. The list is evaluated top to bottom.
  4. Try the rules is not saved anywhere. Enter a zone, a band, orders and logged hours, and it answers which rule decided the day and what it credited — or that no rule matched and the client default applies.
  5. Change the client criteria (hour values, Required hours / day, Off / 30, Good zone ≥ %, Average zone ≥ %, and the default status) and Save client.
  6. Good must be at least Average. A Good of 60 with Average of 70 is refused.
  7. Hours outside 0–24 are refused.
  8. Add client creates a new client, optionally copying another client's starting rules. A duplicate name is refused.
  9. Delete client refuses when riders are still assigned to it, and refuses outright for a system client.
  10. Restore SOP starting rules puts the seeded SOP list back.

F. Settings — Zones & efficiency

  1. The banner repeats that the figures belong to the previous completed month, not the selected one.
  2. Each zone shows Orders · Rider-days · DPD · Target DPD · Efficiency (auto) · Automatic category.
  3. Recompute from last month refreshes the whole table from the previous completed month.
  4. Override the DPD used, the target used or the final category for one zone and press Use. The row gains an Edited tag and the Final category is what the rules read. The automatic figure stays visible beside it.
  5. Re-run Recompute. Your override must survive.
  6. The (no zone) row is synthetic — it can be read but not saved.
  7. Change Good ≥ % / Average ≥ % and confirm the automatic banding of every zone moves accordingly.

G. Change log

  1. Every action above appears in the Change log with When · By · Client · Action · Detail — client created, updated, recomputed, adjusted, reset, month opened.
  2. Make one change and confirm the row carries your own name and the current time.

H. Permissions

  1. A user with payroll.view only: no Off Structure button, no adjustment dialog, no save button in Settings, no Export.
  2. A user with payroll.manage but not payroll.export: can edit, cannot export.
  3. A user with neither: the module is not reachable.

8. How to verify — Rider app (User side)

Payroll is an admin screen only. There is no rider-facing payroll page in this release and no rider app update to publish. The rider side is the input to payroll: what a rider clocks, delivers and asks for. Verify those three inputs land on the admin grid.

  1. Clock-in / clock-out. With a test rider, clock in at a known time and clock out a few hours later. On Admin → Payroll, that rider's Actual Hours for that date must equal the check-in → check-out hours.
  2. Orders. Have the rider submit an order and then verify it on Admin → Deliveries. The verified order must appear as that rider's Final orders for that Kuwait day, and it is the number the client rules read.
  3. Requests. Raise a Leave (or Sick Leave / Accident) request from the app. It shows as Pending in the app. On Admin → Payroll that day must still be unjustified.
  4. Approve the request on Admin → Requests. Come back to Payroll: the day is now OFF (or Sick / Accident), it credits 0 hours, and the rider is no longer counted in Riders with unjustified days.
  5. Precedence, seen from the rider side. Approve the leave, then hand-adjust that same day in the Combined grid. The hand adjustment wins, and the cell keeps the orange marker.
  6. No surprise movement. A client whose rule list has not been saved for the month keeps the previous behaviour — a clocked-in day is a full 12 h day. Confirm on a client you have not configured yet.

9. Notes and limits worth stating

  • No rider app release is required for any of this. The module is admin-only.
  • The month window is this month plus the previous two. That is the existing payroll bound and is unchanged in v4; widening it is a separate decision.
  • Zone efficiency is read from the previous completed month by design, so a newly created zone is Not set until a full month has passed and you press Recompute.
  • One adjustment batch can cover at most 2,000 cells, and one Off Structure upload at most 2,000 rows. Split anything larger.
  • A hand adjustment without a reason cannot be saved — that is the rule that makes the change log auditable.
  • Nothing in this release recalculates a month you have already paid. Past days keep the value they were given; only the rules and bands you apply from now on decide new days.
